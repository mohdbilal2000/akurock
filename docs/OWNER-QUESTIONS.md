# Questions for the business owner

Written 2026-11-10. Each answer changes what the website, the invoices or the SEO work should say. The first four decide everything else.

## Identity and legal

1. **Who is the seller?** The website presents "stonearts® GmbH, Spohrstraße 29/23/1, 1130 Wien" as manufacturer and seller. Invoices are issued by Stone Art Installation, Jaipur. Which legal entity does a customer buy from?
2. **What is the relationship with stonearts® GmbH?** Is there a written agreement allowing use of the stonearts® and Akurock names, the ® marks, their product photos and the Vienna address? If not, this needs resolving before any marketing spend.
3. **"Handcrafted in Austria"** is in the page titles. Where are the panels sold through this site actually made and shipped from?
4. **"4.9 stars from 30 reviews"** is declared to Google on every page. Where are those 30 reviews, and are they for this business?
5. Imprint, privacy policy and terms: whose company details should they carry? EU law requires the real seller's.
6. Is there a business email on the akurock.com domain? The site currently lists a Gmail address.

## Market and offer

7. Which market first: Austria/Germany/Switzerland, Spain, or India? One, to start.
8. Is €220 per panel the real selling price? Does it include VAT? What is the price for India in ₹?
9. Shipping: from where, to which countries, cost, delivery time, who pays duty? The site promises 5–10 business days within Austria.
10. Returns and damage: what is the policy, and who pays return freight for a 2.4 m panel?
11. Stock: are all six finishes available now? Minimum order?
12. Is installation offered, and where?
13. Sample box: price, and is it refunded on order?

## Product facts (needed for the visualizer and the technical page)

14. How many slats does one 600 mm panel have, and what are the slat width and gap? The visualizer draws 9 slats per panel; the real number should replace it.
15. Exact panel size and weight; can panels be cut on site?
16. Is there an acoustic test report (absorption class / NRC) and a fire rating certificate for the panels sold here? Can they be published?
17. Are there other sizes or a horizontal-mount version?

## Sales process

18. Who answers the WhatsApp number (+91 90575 97719), in which languages, and during which hours?
19. What should happen after "Add to cart": online payment, or a quotation by WhatsApp/email? Which payment methods are actually set up?
20. How many enquiries arrive per month now, from where, and how many become orders?
21. Is there a budget for ads, samples to designers, or photography?

## Proof

22. Photos or videos of real installations done by this business, with permission to publish?
23. Customers willing to give a named review?
24. Any architects, designers or showrooms already working with you?
